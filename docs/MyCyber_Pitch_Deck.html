The Problem

Pakistan Has a Data Leak Crisis

🏦
Financial Exposure
Banks, fintechs, and e-commerce companies routinely handle millions of CNICs and IBANs with zero automated detection. A single breach can cost crores in regulatory fines and reputational damage.
⚖️
PDPA Compliance Gap
Pakistan's Personal Data Protection Act is now in force. Law firms, clinics, and CAs face audits — yet 95% of SMEs have no DLP tooling in place. First-mover advantage is available right now.
🤖
The LLM Blind Spot
Staff paste CNICs, API keys, and salary data into ChatGPT and Claude daily. Prompt injection attacks targeting Pakistan-specific data patterns go completely undetected by global tools.
💸
Unaffordable Alternatives
Forcepoint, Symantec, and Microsoft Purview start at $4,000–$15,000/year (PKR 1.1M+). This price point is inaccessible to the 99% of Pakistani businesses that are SMEs.
🌍
No Local Detection
No global DLP tool knows what a Pakistani CNIC, IBAN, or Easypaisa account number looks like. Regex patterns are calibrated for US/EU data formats, producing massive false negatives in Pakistan.
📊
Market Size
Pakistan has 5.2M+ registered businesses. Even 0.1% adopting DLP at PKR 4,500/month represents a PKR 280M/year TAM — growing at 35%+ as PDPA enforcement ramps up.
